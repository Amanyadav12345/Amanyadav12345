### 🏆 Shipped — Clinical Trial Management System

> Finally cooked. After months of wrangling LLMs, RAG pipelines, and regulatory PDFs formatted like it's 1998 — **it's alive.**

A **GenAI-powered platform** for biotech & pharma researchers to stop drowning in paperwork and start actually running trials.

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🧠 **RAG Pipeline** | Mistral-7B + BERT chewing through trial protocols, safety reports & FDA guidelines |
| 📊 **Wearable Dashboard** | Real-time heart rate, sleep & stress data, visualized with Chart.js |
| 🤖 **AI Recommendations** | Fine-tuned Gemini & BERT suggesting safety, recruitment & compliance fixes |
| ⛓️ **Smart Contracts** | Immutable audit trail — because regulators are involved |
| 🌱 **Sustainability Module** | Predicts material waste & environmental impact of clinical site construction |
| 🧑‍⚕️ **CBT AI Therapist** | Mental-health support for participants with real-time sentiment analysis |

A full hospital's worth of AI crammed into one platform. No big deal.
