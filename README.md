## 🏆 Shipped & Done — Clinical Trial Management System

Finally cooked. After months of wrangling LLMs, RAG pipelines, and regulatory PDFs that look like 
they were formatted in 1998 — **it's alive.**

A GenAI-powered platform built for biotech & pharma researchers to stop drowning in paperwork 
and start actually running trials.

- 🧠 **RAG Pipeline** — Mistral-7B + BERT chewing through trial protocols, safety reports & FDA guidelines
- 📊 **Wearable Dashboard** — Real-time heart rate, sleep & stress data visualized beautifully with Chart.js
- 🤖 **AI Recommendations** — Fine-tuned Gemini & BERT suggesting safety measures, recruitment strategies & compliance fixes
- ⛓️ **Smart Contracts** — Because if regulators are involved, you better have an immutable audit trail
- 🌱 **Sustainability Module** — Predicts material waste & environmental impact during clinical site construction
- 🧑‍⚕️ **CBT-Based AI Therapist** — Mental health support for trial participants with real-time sentiment analysis

Basically a full hospital's worth of AI crammed into one platform. No big deal.
